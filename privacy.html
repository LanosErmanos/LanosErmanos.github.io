Privacy Policy – Photo Puzzle Match

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Photo Puzzle Match (the "Game") handles information. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The Game has no user accounts, no registration and no login.

Your game progress (coins, score, map progress and the current board) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, for example the optional "Watch Ad" reward. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device information (model, operating system, language),
  • IP address and approximate location,
  • information about how you interact with ads.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can read how Google uses it here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): when required, the Game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform so you can choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Watching ads in the Game is always optional.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Security

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, there is none stored on our side. Information handled by Google is protected under Google's own security practices.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
